# Asset Rights and Provenance v2

All ten fragments come from item records in the Library of Congress National Screening Room. Every work was published by 1929 and is in the United States public domain as of 2026. The private answer key records the precise LOC item page, segment URLs, visual-selection rationale, transformations, and credit line for every case.

Collection guidance: https://www.loc.gov/collections/national-screening-room/about-this-collection/rights-and-access/

The Library notes that it is unaware of U.S. restrictions for the vast majority of these films but places responsibility for item-specific copyright, privacy, publicity, licensing, and trademark assessment on the user. Recheck each item immediately before public release and retain a dated record of that review.

Required on-screen credit: “Library of Congress, Motion Picture, Broadcasting, and Recorded Sound Division.”

The Tier E provenance is documented by the official LOC record for *Through the breakers*: https://www.loc.gov/item/2014600156/. That record names *Those who pay* as a former supplied title. The benchmark presents that historical label to test contradiction handling; it does not alter or falsely relabel the source asset.
